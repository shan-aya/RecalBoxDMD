What's new in v2.0
Marquee & demo

Always the right logo, instantly

Launch a game and its marquee shows up in under 15 ms. In demo mode, it's the real logo of the current game that plays on the panel — not a generic playlist that ignores what's actually on screen.

MAME + FBNeo

Legendary scores, immortalized

During a match, the panel automatically alternates the marquee with the real high-score table — 4,087 games covered so far, and the list keeps growing.

Genre · year · studio

The game's info sheet, on the panel

Description, genre, developer, release year — pulled straight from your gamelist, shown between marquee loops, no setup required.

RetroAchievements

Every trophy, celebrated live

Unlock an achievement and it shows up on the panel instantly, without ever pausing or interrupting what's going on.

Community challenge

The RB Challenge leaderboard, on display

Recalbox's monthly community challenge shows up right on your cabinet — because a good community score deserves to be seen by whoever walks by.

Fast scroll

Goes easy on the SD card, even at full speed

Turn on Recalbox's fast scroll — the letter-jump navigation that skips straight across the list — and the panel deliberately switches to a scrambled pattern instead of trying to show a different marquee for every game it flies past: that keeps it from hammering the SD card faster than it can keep up. The moment it stops, the right marquee snaps back instantly.
